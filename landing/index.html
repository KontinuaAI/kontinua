How It Works

From boundary conditions
to prediction in 3 steps

No HPC cluster. No weeks of compute. No PhDs required.

01

Upload Your Conditions

Upload initial states, boundary conditions, or geometry parameters in HDF5, NetCDF, VTK, or OpenFOAM format. Our pipeline normalises and validates automatically.

02

Select Physics Domain

Choose from 16 pre-trained physics domains — turbulence, CFD, MHD, acoustic scattering, reaction-diffusion, astrophysics. Our model router selects the optimal architecture for your problem.

03

Get Predictions

Receive spatiotemporal field predictions with VRMSE, spectral MSE, and Wasserstein metrics in milliseconds. Export rollout videos, xarray datasets, or raw tensors.

Python SDK · 5 lines to your first prediction 
import simforge

# Authenticate with your API key
client = simforge.Client(api_key="sf_live_...")

# Upload boundary conditions + get prediction
result = client.predict(
    domain="turbulence",
    input_path="./initial_state.hdf5",
    model="cnext-unet-v2",       # top-performing architecture
    rollout_steps=100,
)

# Access predictions with full metrics
print(result.vrmse)          # 0.023
print(result.latency_ms)     # 47ms
result.export("./output.hdf5")
